Method: median of asking rents weighted by proximity (weight = 1 / (0.25 + distance)), minus the negotiation discount. The recommended rent targets that market rent minus the retention margin. It never falls below the current rent indexed to CPI and never exceeds the market rent, which is the ceiling a First-tier Tribunal would apply if the tenant challenged it. In England, rent can go up only once a year, using a Section 13 notice with two months' notice.
